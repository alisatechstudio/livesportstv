Frequently Asked Inquiries

Why do some streams say "Stream Unavailable or Offline"?

Many public TV channels only broadcast active HLS feeds during live sporting fixtures. Others enforce geographic geo-blocking (restricting viewers outside their domestic territory).

Can I submit my sports channel or public stream to the directory?

Yes! We welcome suggestions for free-to-air sports channels. Please email us the channel name, country of origin, official website, and public M3U8 feed URL.

What is the typical response turnaround?

Our volunteer team reviews submissions daily. We typically process inquiries and DMCA notices within 24 to 48 business hours.
