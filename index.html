About LiveSportsTV

Our Mission

LiveSportsTV was established to make discovering legal, free-to-air international sports broadcasts simple, fast, and accessible for sports enthusiasts worldwide. We curate television channels from over 175 countries into a streamlined, high-performance web directory.

How We Curate & Index

Our platform aggregates verified public stream playlists maintained by the global open-source community (iptv-org). We continuously categorize channels by country, spoken language, and sporting disciplines, ensuring users can find local and international sports telecasts without complex software configurations.

Technology & Standards

LiveSportsTV is built purely on modern web technologies (HTML5 Video, HLS.js, Tailwind CSS) prioritizing fast load times, accessibility, clean aesthetics, and minimal resource usage across desktop, mobile, and connected television devices.
