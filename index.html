❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Live Sports TV

Everything you need to know about streaming live sports TV channels, legal compliance, and device playback.

Is LiveSportsTV completely free and legal to use?

Yes, LiveSportsTV is 100% free with no subscription or account required. We index publicly broadcasted free-to-air HLS television streams legally published on the open internet.

Which sports can I watch on LiveSportsTV?

You can browse verified channels broadcasting football (soccer), cricket, motorsport (Formula, GT, rally), basketball, tennis, combat sports (boxing, MMA), golf, rugby, baseball, hockey, and Olympic athletics.

Does LiveSportsTV host or store any video on its servers?

No. LiveSportsTV is purely a search directory and indexing interface. We do not host, store, transcode, or re-transmit media files. All streams connect directly from the original broadcaster's content servers.

Can I watch live sports TV on mobile devices and Smart TVs?

Yes. LiveSportsTV runs natively on modern HTML5 web browsers across iOS (Safari), Android (Chrome), Windows, macOS, Linux, and Smart TV browsers with zero external app downloads.

Why do some channels display a 403 Forbidden error?

A 403 error typically indicates that the broadcaster restricts the stream to specific geographic regions (geo-blocking) or only activates the stream during live match broadcasts.

How often is the channel list updated?

Our channel index is automatically updated daily from verified open-source IPTV databases, ensuring dead channels are flagged and new sports channels are added.

How can copyright owners request removal of a stream listing?

Copyright holders can submit a DMCA takedown notice to support@livesportstv.store. We process and de-index verified requests within 24-48 business hours.
