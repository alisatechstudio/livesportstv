Technical Architecture

How HTTP Live Streaming (HLS) Works on LiveSportsTV

Learn how our platform delivers live sports broadcasts in-browser without third-party plugins or servers:

📡 Direct Origin Delivery

The video stream is transmitted directly from the broadcaster's origin server or content delivery network (CDN) to your browser via HTML5 Video or HLS.js. LiveSportsTV does not act as a relay proxy.

🛡️ Zero Intermediate Hosting

LiveSportsTV does not store, transcode, encode, or re-broadcast media. All streaming packets remain strictly under the original broadcaster's content management rules and authorization.

📱 Universal Device Support

Using native HLS in Safari and iOS alongside adaptive bitrate HLS.js in Chrome, Firefox, and Edge, our player dynamically adjusts stream resolution to your network bandwidth.

🔒 Geo-Availability & Licensing

Broadcasters may restrict HLS access based on geographical IP addresses or only activate transmission during scheduled sporting events, which can occasionally trigger 403 Forbidden statuses.
